Dec 24, 2024

**Dear Advisors, Observers, Interns and Loyal Customers,**

As the Christmas season brings us closer to our loved ones, Remilia Corporation extends warm wishes for a time of connection and reflection. To reflect on Remilia's own 2024, among a plethora of memes, happenings and drama (lore-generating events), we saw:

- The release of our first publications with [_Remilia Quarterly Vol. 1_](https://house.remilia.org/products/remilia-quarterly-vol-1-ss24?ref=blog.remilia.org) and [_CHINA!_](https://house.remilia.org/products/china-by-c-fang-et-al-2024?ref=blog.remilia.org),
- The first of our on-going collaboration with [FRUiTS Magazine](https://www.instagram.com/fruitsmag/?ref=blog.remilia.org) with the [3D FRUiTS Milady collection](https://fruits.remilia.org/?ref=blog.remilia.org) and the tie-in [Kagami Academy](https://docs.scatter.art/blog/fruits-x-remilia/?ref=blog.remilia.org) auctioncore release,
- Our [rave community](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KsEcA151hFk&ref=blog.remilia.org) branching out into Korea and Japan with events partnered with [DIG SHUBIYA](https://digshibuya.com/en/?ref=blog.remilia.org) and [FRUiTS Magazine](https://www.instagram.com/fruitsmag/?ref=blog.remilia.org),
- The opening of [Miladychan](https://miladychan.org/?ref=blog.remilia.org) introducing the realtime imageboard as our first dedicated community forum,
- On-going improvements to [Miladycraft](https://miladycraft.net/?ref=blog.remilia.org) (the minimum viable metaverse™),
- The release of [Megasize Alien Milady Fumo](https://x.com/MiladyMaker333/status/1814784025426600429?ref=blog.remilia.org) dolls and [3D Milady Fumo](https://opensea.io/collection/milady-fumo-baby-404?ref=blog.remilia.org),
- The introduction of [#CHEESEWORLD](https://x.com/hashtag/CHEESEWORLD?ref=blog.remilia.org) into the memetic pantheon over a fond summer of TGE delay,
- And, of course, the first [Beetle Game achievements round](https://achievements.remilia.org/?ref=blog.remilia.org) and [CULT ICO](https://cult.inc/?ref=blog.remilia.org), the much anticipated umbrella product of our hyperfinancialized attention line.

But, really, 2024 already feels like yesterday... and 2025 almost feels like the first year of Remilia all over again.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/34/4d/344db379-6ee0-4527-979b-c712c2e2f368/content/images/2025/09/image.png)

It’s been a long ride to the top since 2021, and as NFT's perennial underdog—an outlier among blue-chip teams by having entirely avoided any private investment (among other differences, too...)—CULT’s launch has for the first time left us positioned with the real capital runway necessary to expand our team and take each of our emerging culture and tech ventures into hyperdrive.

**This is a new era for Remilia.**

In the coming months, you’ll see us deliver new offerings in culturally relevant industries as we spread out our reach, and new AI-oriented applets under a general mandate of accelerating meme making and meme distribution, in our bid for total mindshare domination—virtually and IRL.

We also begin serious development of Remilia Chat in parallel, retaining a fully staffed development team to turn our myriad proof-of-concepts into reality, and achieve the long-term network accelerationist, social exocortex vision of [Remilia’s New Internet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TEBG82-NyVQ&&ref=blog.remilia.org).

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/34/4d/344db379-6ee0-4527-979b-c712c2e2f368/content/images/2025/09/image-1.png)

To give some insight on the internal corporate machine: next month, we’re excited to open our first physical US office in [Irvine, California](https://thebigplanbook.com/blog/the-man-with-the-plan?ref=blog.remilia.org), ending remote work and bringing the core product, marketing and engineering teams together in-person.

After much deliberation, we settled not on NYC, not Austin, not Miami, and not San Francisco, but a short flight away to SoCal: Irvine is America’s cleanest and safest city, [an unsung masterpiece of new urbanism](https://placesjournal.org/article/discovering-irvine/?ref=blog.remilia.org) as the world’s most successful master-planned charter city—with accessible connectivity to LA by car, SF domestically and Asia internationally (another bet, on the seat of culture rotating Westward & Asia-bound).

Here in Irvine CA, we'll attract talent to expand our development studio, including with an in-house AI research lab.

We will also be expanding our existing office in Hongdae, Seoul, which houses our growing fashion, design and illustration teams.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/34/4d/344db379-6ee0-4527-979b-c712c2e2f368/content/images/2025/09/image-2.png)

We knew from the start Remilia is playing a decade long game to win the culture war, and it’s this coming year we go on the offensive.

The macro timing could not be more perfect, as the Trump Mandate vibe shift confirmed our out-of-the-money bets placed on the outside edges of the Overton Window will pay off: the pendulum has now swung back into our corner, leaving the cancelled to inherit the earth... just as prophesied.

But for now, let’s take the time to set down arms and enjoy peace this Christmas. May this time strengthen our bond and connection that we all share across the network.

Here's to abundance, love and victory in the coming year—and to a thousand year dynasty beyond 🥂.

**Merry Christmas from your dear friends at the Remilia Corporation,**

**Charlotte Fang**

CEO @ Remilia Corporation
